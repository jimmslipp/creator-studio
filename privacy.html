Creator Studio privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Creator Studio is a private desktop app used by one family to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

What it uses

With your permission, it uses Google's YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic details of your own channel (name and video list).

Where data is kept

Everything stays on the family's own Windows PC. The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on that PC only. No data is sent to, stored on or shared with any other server or person, and nothing is sold.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or with "Disconnect" in the app.

Creator Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

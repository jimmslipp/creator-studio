Creator Studio privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Creator Studio is a private desktop app used by one family to share videos to their own YouTube channel and their own TikTok account.

What it uses

YouTube: with your permission, it uses Google's YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic details of your own channel (name and video list).

TikTok: with your permission, it uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to read your basic profile (display name and avatar) and to send videos you have approved to your own TikTok account, as drafts for you to finish, or as posts. If you choose, it reads the view, like and share counts of your own videos so the app can suggest better clips.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on the family's own Windows PC. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that PC only. No data is sent to, stored on or shared with any other server or person, apart from the videos you choose to send to YouTube or TikTok, and nothing is sold. No advertising or tracking.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (YouTube), in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services (TikTok), or with "Disconnect" in the app. Disconnecting deletes the stored token from the PC.

Creator Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of TikTok's services follows the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.
